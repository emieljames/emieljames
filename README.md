## 🚀 Featured projects

### 💠 Kumpirma: signature verification & authentication
> Document integrity through a hybrid AI + blockchain pipeline.

- **Restoration:** GANs reconstruct and denoise low-quality signature scans
- **Verification:** Capsule Networks preserve stroke orientation and spatial structure
- **Proof:** verification metadata is anchored on **Ethereum**, with files on **IPFS**, for a tamper-proof audit trail

`Python` `PyTorch` `OpenCV` `Solidity` `IPFS` `Next.js`

### 🩸 [RedQuest](https://github.com/emieljames/redquest): blood donor mobilization
> Real-time coordination for emergency blood supply.

- **Live coordination** between donors and requesters over WebSockets
- **Route optimization** with mapping APIs to reach donors faster

`Next.js` `Laravel` `Node.js` `Firebase` `Google Maps API`

### 🏠 Toka: household tasks meet financial literacy
> A micro-incentive app that links chores to allowance.

- Kids complete tasks and earn allowance automatically, which teaches saving and budgeting early

`React Native` `Firebase` `Node.js`
